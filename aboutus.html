Who We Are

Electro Badr was born in Casablanca in 1983 as a specialist watch house — a family business built on one simple belief: every Moroccan family deserves access to quality timepieces at honest prices.

"We don't just sell electronics — we build relationships that last generations."

— Badr, Founder
